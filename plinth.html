plinth

offline desktop audio player for macos and linux. it plays the music files on your computer, with no account and no streaming.

code

features

  • browse the library by artist, album or track
  • sort track lists by clicking a column header
  • build a queue, reorder it and see recently played tracks
  • shuffle, repeat, favorites and volume memory
  • playlists, saved on your computer
  • now playing screen and a popout player tinted by the cover art
  • light and dark themes

stack

  • app tauri 2
  • interface sveltekit, typescript
  • audio rust, rodio, symphonia
